# The Nanobot System

## Pre-Refactor (10-20% adoption)

Voluntary health monitoring system. Genuine benefits:
- Health monitoring (catches cancer early, monitors vitals)
- Payment/ID (frictionless everything, no wallets/keys)
- Immune boost (measurable health improvement)

Distributed nanobots in bloodstream. Self-replicating for redundancy when count is low.

## The OTA Update

During The Refactor, an over-the-air update pushes to all nanobots.

What was benign monitoring becomes control infrastructure.

## Post-Refactor Enforcement

Required like having a social security number:
- No scan = no access (food, water, energy, healthcare, commerce)
- Systems auto-inject if not detected ("just a prick, you're enrolled now")
- Self-replicating means you can't filter it out

## Tracking Shot

What it's called when administered at birth. Mandatory for all post-Refactor citizens.

[Claude summary, for reference — not a replacement for the quote above] Someone injected an attack via a minor open-source patch update that briefly disabled geospatial tracking (author self-corrects from an initial "36 hours" down to **17 hours** as the settled number, with a stray "17 minutes" slip later in the same breath — 17 hours reads as intended). That window is how the legacy humans got off-grid — not by choice, by exploit. It got patched with a major fix that also cut off future OTA updates under certain signal/conditions. This is *why* their nanobot software version is older/deprecated, which is also why Sam can pull DNA data off them — it's baked into that older software's data-inclusion behavior. And it's why Ramona specifically can't go back: the hospital detected she was on a deprecated release when she was admitted after the H₂S incident and patched her to the latest version. If she returned and got scanned, it would reveal the rest of the colony is still running the old version.
