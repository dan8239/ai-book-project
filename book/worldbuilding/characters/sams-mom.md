## What Is Known

She would not have left the kids by choice. Sam knows this. Whatever happened to her happened during or because of his father's decline — the drinking, the loss of identity, the person his father became after the job was gone.

She was, by Sam's account, a saint. The contrast between who his father became and who she was is part of what makes the loss so specifically painful. He lost the wrong parent.

## Story Role

- **Don't explain early**: Sam doesn't talk about her. Her absence is felt, not described.
- **Nan as surrogate**: Nan filled the role. The warmth Sam has for Nan is partly love for Nan, partly grief for his mother.
- **Ramona parallel**: Sam's mother stayed present and was consumed by it. Sam worries, somewhere below articulation, that Ramona is becoming that. He's repeating the pattern from the other side.
- **The reveal**: When it comes, it should recontextualize everything we know about why Sam is the way he is.
