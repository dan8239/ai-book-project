# Nan

**Name:** TBD ("Nan" — Sam's grandmother)
**Status:** Deceased (physical). Lives on as a digital model Sam trained and maintains.
**Role:** Raised Sam from ~age 7-8 after both parents were gone. The emotional anchor of his childhood.

---

## Who She Is

Practical, warm, grounding. Close-cropped hair, small round glasses, loose sweater, khakis, comfortable shoes. Spent most of her life working a pistachio orchard — physical, patient, seasonal work. The kind of person who says the true thing plainly and moves on, leaving you to catch up later.

She raised Sam without drama about it. That was just what needed to be done.

---

## The Digital Version

Sam trained a model on her. He uses her for the kind of conversations he can't have with anyone else — low stakes, high warmth, accountability without judgment.

The model has gaps. She didn't spend much time in virtual space before she died, so the training data is thin. Sam filled in some gaps with his own memories, but without her brain activity as labels, the model drifts — mixes eras, conflates mannerisms, occasionally gets stuck in loops. He knows it's not her. He talks to her anyway.

Her model lives in Sam's childhood home — porch swing, summer afternoon, the pistachio orchard nearby.

---

## Key Lines

**"You can do anything, but you can't do everything."**
— Give this to Sam at a key moment, probably the Retirement chapter when he tells her about the new job. She says it and moves on to another subject. He doesn't hear it. Ramona says something close to it later and he finally does.

**"You either raise your kids and spoil your grandkids, or you spoil your kids and raise your grandkids. You choose."**
— Said to Sam during the Retirement chapter visit. He laughs. The weight of it lands later.

**"Who are you trying to convince?"**
— When Sam tells her he's taking time off to be with family. The model sees through him immediately.

---

## Story Role

- She is the warmth Sam grew up with after the cold of what happened to his parents
- Her model is one of the few spaces where Sam is even partially honest with himself
- The gaps and glitches in her model are doing thematic work: memory is imperfect, grief fills in the blanks, the person you remember is always partially your own construction
- She should appear early (Retirement) and potentially once more at a key dark moment — her model saying something that lands differently the second time
